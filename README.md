# Sales Dashboard PowerBI
This sales dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance across multiple dimensions. The total sales amount to 2.30M, with a profit of 286.4K, discounts totaling 1.56K, and 38K units sold. Sales have increased steadily over the years, from 0.48M in 2011 to 0.73M in 2014. Phones and Chairs are the top-performing sub-categories, each contributing around 0.33M in sales, while Envelopes and Art bring in the least. Region-wise, the East leads with 725.46K (31.58%) in sales, followed by the Central (678.78K) and South (501.24K) regions. In terms of category performance, Technology generates the highest revenue at 0.84M, with Furniture and Office Supplies trailing closely at 0.74M and 0.72M, respectively. The overall trend suggests consistent growth, particularly in the Technology category, which also aligns with the increasing trend in sub-category sales. This dashboard is useful for identifying sales hotspots and guiding strategic planning.
